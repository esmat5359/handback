# Handover inventory - photo kit loan

Recorded by the owner and acknowledged by the holder at handover. Every item is
labelled I1 to I5. The condition written here is the agreed starting point.

I1: Mirrorless camera body - no dents on the body, rear screen unscratched, shutter and
    buttons all work.
I2: 24-70mm zoom lens - front and rear glass clean with no scratches, zoom ring turns
    smoothly, lens caps present.
I3: Carbon-fibre tripod - one small paint chip on the lower left leg (already there before
    this loan), all leg locks hold firmly, head pans and tilts smoothly.
I4: Flash unit - fires on test, battery cover intact, mounting foot undamaged.
I5: Camera bag - clean inside and out, every zipper runs smoothly, shoulder strap sound.
